📣 Shabpa 3.17 is out

🆕 What's new
• 🎨 All 30 premium themes were redrawn: instead of simple shapes, a full painting with moving parts (snow, flames, fish, rain, lightning...)
• 🛍 The theme store shows each theme's painting on its tile

🛠 Fixes
• 🔤 Text stays readable over the theme paintings (sidebar, page title, footer and cards)
• 🪞 The theme paintings were mirrored in Persian mode; the koi swam tail first
• 🪟 The overlay no longer locks the whole app: it used to wait for the graphics driver while finding the monitor, and the window sometimes stopped for up to 30 seconds
• ⏱ Closing the app and changing the language no longer wait 3 to 5 seconds
• 🏅 The Medals page opens about 4 times faster

💡 Lighting
• Every device has its own separate road to the lighting server; a slow device (such as memory or the motherboard) no longer holds back fans and LED strips
• Memory and devices the lighting server cannot see are still not controlled
